\# Aura



\### A private reflective space for putting things down, keeping what matters, and noticing yourself over time.



Aura is a personal reflection product designed around a simple idea: not everything needs to be fixed, explained, or turned into advice.



It gives people a private place to express what is on their mind, keep what matters, and notice how their thoughts change or stay with them over time.



\## The idea



Aura is built around three actions:



\*\*Express → Record → Reflect\*\*



\- \*\*Express\*\* — put a thought down in whatever form it comes.

\- \*\*Record\*\* — keep what matters without overwriting the original.

\- \*\*Reflect\*\* — notice patterns, changes, and things that stay with you over time.



\## Product areas



\- \*\*Today\*\* — a gentle place to arrive.

\- \*\*Express\*\* — write or speak freely and keep the entry.

\- \*\*Weekly Reflect\*\* — notice what has been coming back lately.

\- \*\*Record\*\* — return to things you've left behind.

\- \*\*Long-term Reflect\*\* — notice what stayed and how your perspective changed.

\- \*\*Personal Suggestions\*\* — keep useful things you've shared and allow them to inform optional suggestions.



\## Design principles



Aura was designed around a few boundaries:



\- Your words remain the source of truth.

\- Reflection is optional.

\- Prompts can always be skipped.

\- Nothing is overwritten.

\- Insights notice; they never diagnose.

\- Colour carries interface role, not emotional value.

\- Depth comes through space, lightness, and layering rather than darkness.

\- The interface should never compete with what someone came to say.
